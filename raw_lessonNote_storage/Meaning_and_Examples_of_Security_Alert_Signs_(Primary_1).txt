Revision and Weekly Assessment (Test) LEARNING OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. explain the meaning of security alert. 2. identify security alert signs. 3. discuss the importance of security alert. LEARNING BEHAVIOURS Some of the pupils can identify some of the security signs. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of chart showing different security alert signs. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 2 – INTRODUCTION MEANING OF SECURITY ALERTS Security alerts are signals send to inform people of the danger ahead. MEANING OF SECURITY ALERT SIGNS Security Alert Signs are tools used to indicate danger ahead. EXAMPLES OF SECURITY ALERT SIGNS Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Display charts showing security alert signs for pupils to identify and describe. 1. Traffic signs 2. Beware of Dogs 3. Limbs May Fall 4. No entry – Authorized Persons Only 5. High Voltage 6. Use Hamlet 7. Car Seat Belt Alarm 8. Fire Alarm 9. Danger zone 10. No smoking 11. Turn Off Engine or Handset 10. Keep Out of Reach of Children and Pets, etc. CONSEQUENCES OF NEGLECTING SECURITY ALERT SIGNS Consequence means something that happens when you do something in the wrong way. The security alert signs are to inform us about the danger ahead. It will be very dangerous to neglect the warning. The consequences of neglecting the security alert or sign are as follows: 1. Accident 2. Arrest and punishment 3. Endanger other people 4. Permanent or temporary injury. For r example – dog bit, burn, electric shock, etc. 5. Embarrassment or insult 6. Death LESSON 2 – TRAFFIC LIGHT Traffic light is the signal used to control the free movement of traffic. They are placed on roads. There different colors of lights that tell the drivers what to do. The red colour means STOP . Red light tells drivers to stop. The yellow colour means READY TO GO Yellow light tells drivers to stop when it is safe to, because the light is about to turn red. The green colour means GO Green light tells the drivers to start driving or keep driving. IMPORTANCE OF TRAFFIC SIGNS The importance of traffic signs are as follows: 1. It controls movements. 2. It conducts orderly flow of movement. 3. It reduces road accidents. 4. The traffic lights make the road safer for the users. LESSON 3 – REVISION AND WEEKLY ASSESSMENT (TEST) PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. Teacher revises the previous lesson based on the pupil’s related knowledge or understanding. 2. Teacher organizes pupils in groups or pairs depending on the size of the class and available instructional materials. 3. Teacher displays charts showing most of the sign alert signs. 4. Teacher lets the pupils identify and describe the signs. For example, sign with dog and text (beware of dog). Pupil’s Activities – Identify and explain the security sign alert. 5. Teacher uses the pupils relevant responses to introduce the lesson. Pupil’s Activities – Learners pay attention to the lesson introduction to understand the concept of the lesson. 6. Teacher leads a discussion on the meaning of security sign alert with appropriate examples. Pupil’s Activities – Learners participate actively in the class discussion. 7. Teacher uses chart to explain meaning and importance traffic light signs. Pupil’s Activities – Learners state the function of each traffic colour and analyze their importance on the roads. 8. Teacher guides pupils to identify and state the consequences of neglecting security sign or alert. Pupil’s Activities – Learners analyze the consequences of neglecting the security signs or alert in their communities. 9. Teacher summarizes the lesson on the board by asking appropriate questions. Pupil’s Activities – Learners participate actively in the summary of the lesson by responding correctly to the questions and write as instructed. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
