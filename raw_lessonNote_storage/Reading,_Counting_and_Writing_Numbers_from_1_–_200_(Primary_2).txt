To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Displays some collections of different objects. 3. Lets pupils identify each of objects. 4. Leads to count the number of different collections. Pupil’s Activities – Identify and count each of the collection. 5. Display flash cards representing the total number of each collection. 6. Ask pupils to match each card number with respective number of collection. Pupil’s Activities – Match the card number with the appropriate number of each collection. 7. Displays chart showing whole number 1 – 50, 50 – 100 and 101 – 200. 8. Helps the pupils to recognize, read and write up to 200. Pupil’s Activities – Recognize, read and write up to 200. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
