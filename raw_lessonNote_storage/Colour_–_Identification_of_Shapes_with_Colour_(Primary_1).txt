Identification of Colour 2. Identification of Shapes with Colours PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – 1. identify at least four colours. 2. colour objects with various colour. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Different colours – red, blue, yellow, green, etc. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Paper Crayons Coloured pen Colour charts METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON ONE – INTRODUCTION IDENTIFICATION OF DIFFERENT SHAPES WITH COLOURS Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Displays different shapes with different colours. First – asks the pupils, what shape is this? Writes the pupil’s points on the board. 1. Balloon 2. ____________________ 3. ____________________ 4. ____________________ 5. ____________________, etc. Secondly, asks the pupils, what colour is this shape? Writes the pupil’s points on the board. 1. Red balloon 2. ____________________ 3. ____________________ 4. ____________________ 5. ____________________, etc. LESSON EVALUATION/ASSIGNMENT Find out the colour of the following – 1. Grass 2. Soil 3. Goat 4. Commercial bus 5. Police uniform 6. Army uniform, etc. LESSON TWO – IDENTIFICATION OF LOGO WITH COLOURS Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Asks to identify National Symbol and its colours. Pupil’s Expected Response – Nigerian or National Flag. Green White Green IDENTIFY COLOUR OF THEIR SCHOOL UNIFORM AND BUILDING Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Asks the pupils, what is the colour of their uniform? LESSON EVALUATION/ASSIGNMENT Draw and Colour National Flag PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Guides pupils to identify objects through colours – living and non living things. Pupil’s Activities – Identify objects through colours – living and non living things. 3. Guides pupils to draw and colour some objects. Pupil’s Activities – Draw some objects and colour them e.g. ball, cup,box etc. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Uses of Colour (Primary 1) LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to: 1. name three types of colour. 2. colour two types of object.
