Prepare home made polish and different types of cleaning agents. 2. Use the cleaning agents properly. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Kerosene/turpentine, candle wax, bar soap and palm oil. 2. Pawpaw, pineapple leaves, fine sand, detergent, grater, sauce pan, grind egg shell, wood ash, lime, and salt. 3. Wooden furniture, table, chairs, cupboards, shelves, cloth poles. 4. Galvanised iron, saucepan, pots, buckets, cutlery, water. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTION Home made polish are polish make with local materials and used for cleaning furniture at home. Home made cleaning agents are materials used for cleaning the household articles and equipments. PREPARATION OF HOME MADE FURNITURE POLISH Materials needed for making home made polish and cleaning agents are – 1. Kerosene 2. Candles 3. Bar soaps 4. Boiling water Procedures – 1. Gale the candle and soap 2. Place the grate mixture in an earth ware pot (or enamel pot) and add boiling water. 3. Stand the pot in boiling water and allow the content to melt. 4. Beat the method mixture thoroughly into a cream using a wooden spoon. 5. Add turpentine gradually and keep beating until the polish is perfectly blended. 6. Store in a good container and well cover. PREPARATION OF HOME MADE CLEANING AGENTS Materials needed for making home made cleaning agents are – 1. Egg shell 2. Detergent 3. Broken China ware Procedures – 1. Grind the egg shell and piece of China ware into fine powder. 2. Sift the mixture to smooth powder. 3. Mix the powder thoroughly with detergent powder. 4. Store in a covered container. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. Revises the previous lesson based on the previous lesson or pupil’s knowledge. 2. Displays sample home made polish and cleaning agents. 3. Lets pupils examine and state their uses. Pupil’s Activities – State the uses of home made polish and cleaning agents. 4. Uses the samples to introduce the lesson. 5. Provides all materials need for making home made polish and cleaning agents. Pupil’s Activities – Listen to the lesson introduce and identify each of the materials displayed. 6. Uses the materials to prepares different samples of home made furniture polish and cleaning agents and displays in class. Pupil’s Activities – Examine samples of home made furniture polish and cleaning agents displayed. 7. Leads pupils to itemised the steps in the production of home made polish and cleaning agent. Pupil’s Activities – State the steps of making polish and cleaning agents following the steps. Practice the steps of making polish and cleaning agents following the steps. 8. Demonstrates making of home made furniture polish and cleaning agents. Pupil’s Activities – Make home made polish and cleaning agents. Display the products made. 9. Summarizes the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as instructed. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Guidelines for the Uses of Home Cleaning Agents LESSON EVALUATION Ask pupils to – 1. State the materials used for making home made polish. 2. State the materials used for making home made cleaning agents. 3. List steps involved in the production of home made furniture polish. 4. List steps involved in the production of home made cleaning agents.
