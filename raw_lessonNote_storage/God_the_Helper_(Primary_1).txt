INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Pictures of manner sent from heaven, water comes out of the rock, etc. Read and understand these Bible passages: Genesis 1:7; Exodus 17:6; Numbers 11:32; 20:7 – 13; 1 Kings 17:1 – 6; and John 6:10 – 13; 7:18 – 24. METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. Related Posts CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTION Tells the pupils the story of how God created man and give food and water to the Israelites in the Bible. Also, how Jesus feed 5,000 men with 5 breads and 2 fish. WAYS IN WHICH GOD HELPS US God is our creator and help us in many ways. He gave us – Life Food Water WHY GOD HELPS US God helps us because: He is our Father and our creator. We cannot help ourselves. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; Guides the pupils to mention who created them. Pupil’s Activities – Mention God as their creator. Asks them to mention who created the food we eat and water we drink. Guides the pupils to discuss what happened to us if there is no food and water. Pupil’s Activities – Discuss with the class what happened if there is no food and water. Leads them to mention ways God helps us. Pupil’s Activities – List ways in which God help us. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – God help the Israelites. LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to: Why does God help us? In which ways did God help the people of Israel when they were in desert places? What did Jesus use to feed the five thousand people? In which way has God helped you?
