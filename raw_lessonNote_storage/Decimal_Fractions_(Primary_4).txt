LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: identify decimal fractions up to tenths and hundredths. convert common fractions to decimal fractions. convert decimal fractions to common fractions. RATIONALE LEARNING MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of tenths and hundredths squares charts.
