Means of Transportation in Nigeria 2. Travelling by Air for the First Time 3. On Board an Engine Boat 4. A Journey by Lorry PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. use the words and sentences structures connected with air, water and road transport systems. 2. use the words in sentences of their own. 3. practice skimming for ideas of a passage. 4. do oral and written comprehension. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Most of the pupils can read and write. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Course books 2. Supplementary readers 3. Pictures of travelers. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON FOCUS TOPICS Choose one of the Topics – 1. Means of Transportation in Nigeria 2. Travelling by Air for the First Time 3. On Board an Engine Boat 4. A Journey by Lorry PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Uses words and sentence situations connected with different transport systems. 3. Guides pupils to discuss different transport systems. Pupil’s Activities – Active participation. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – English Studies (Primary 4) LESSON EVALUATION Ask pupils to: 1. use the words and sentences structures connected with air, water and road transport systems. 2. do oral and written comprehension. 3. reading the passages.
