Introductory Activities – Exercise 2. When to take Exercise PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – know when to take exercise. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR The pupils can perform basic exercise. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Materials for doing some exercises e.g. sportswear. 2. Pictures or chart showing people doing some exercises. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON WHEN TO TAKE EXERCISE 1. Regularly before sunrise. (in the morning). 2. Regularly after the sunset (in the evening). PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Leads pupils to the field. 3. Ask pupils about the atmospheric condition – whether cold, cool, warm or hot. Pupil’s Activities – Identify the present condition of the atmosphere. 4. Uses the weather conditions to discuss the appropriate time to take exercise. Pupil’s Activities – Participate in the class discussion. 3. Summarizes the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as instructed. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
