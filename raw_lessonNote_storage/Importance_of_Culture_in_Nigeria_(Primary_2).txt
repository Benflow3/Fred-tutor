Introductory Activities 2. Meaning of Culture 3. Importance of Culture in Nigeria PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – state the importance of culture in Nigeria. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Ways of life of people. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Cloth and food wear and eat by different people in the society. METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Asks the pupils to define culture with appropriate examples. Pupil’s Expected Response – Culture is the people’s way of life. The ways they dress, eat, dance, etc. IMPORTANCE OF CULTURE IN NIGERIA 1. It distinguishes man from animals. 2. It provides solutions to human problems. 3. It shapes behavior. 4. It keeps social relationships intact. 5. It determines our present situation. For example, foood we have to eat, etc. 6. It provides sense of belongings. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Guides pupils to identify the importance of culture. Pupil’s Activities – Identify the importance of culture. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
