Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. recite the Surah correctly. 2. mention the difficult words and repeat their pronunciation correctly. 3. commit the Surah to memory. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR The pupils INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of Chart containing different alphabets with vowel sounds. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – ARABIC TEXT OF SURATUL – KAUTHAR إِنَّا أَعْطَيْنَاكَ الْكَوْثَرَ فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَانْحَرْ إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْأَبْتَرُ LESSON 2 – REPEAT THE LESSON 1 PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Writes the Arabic text of the Surah on the cardboard and fix it on the board. 3. Recites the Surah slowly. Pupil’s Activities – Listen attentively to the teacher’s recitation. Repeat the Surah after the teacher. Recite the Surah in group and individually. 4. Listens to pupils’ recitation and identifies the mispronounced words. 5. Repeats the mispronounced words many times. Pupil’s Activities – Repeat the mispronounced words after the teacher. Recite the Surah off head. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. NEXT LESSON LESSON EVALUATION Ask pupils to: 1. recite the Surah correctly. 2. mention the difficult words and repeat their pronunciation correctly. 3. commit the Surah to memory.
