Revision/Short Note and Weekly Assessment TIME – 40 Minutes each. LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: 1. Explain the meaning of drugs. 2. Classify drugs as either naturally occurring drugs or synthetic drugs. 3. List examples each of naturally occurring drugs and synthetic drugs RATIONALE A drug is any substance that can affects the operations of the body. The effects can be beneficial or harmful to health. Some drugs are used in the treatment, prevention and cure of a disease and are called medicines. In this lesson, the pupils will understand different kinds of drug and choose actively. LEARNING MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of sample and chart of synthetic and naturally occurring drugs. PREVIOUS KNOWLEDGE Drugs are some called medicine. Both synthetic and naturally occurring drugs are basically drugs used by people. The pupils have the knowledge of these drugs. LESSON DEVELOPMENT 4 – REVISION AND SHORT NOTE INTRODUCTION MEANING OF DRUGS Drug is any substance that affects the action of the body. That drugs are used as medicine to treat or prevent sicknesses. These substances can be in form of solid (tablets), liquid (tonics, injections, creams or drips) and gas (inhalers) TYPES OF DRUG There are two types of drug – synthetic and naturally occurring drugs. SYNTHETIC DRUGS These are produced by mixing chemical compounds together e.g. cough syrup, panadol, etc. These are usually produced by pharmaceutical companies under licence. NATURALLY OCCURRING DRUGS Naturally occurring drugs e.g. honey, salt, alcohol, caffeine in Kolanut, bitter leaf, palm wine, etc. These drugs occur in nature. They are products of plants or animals.
