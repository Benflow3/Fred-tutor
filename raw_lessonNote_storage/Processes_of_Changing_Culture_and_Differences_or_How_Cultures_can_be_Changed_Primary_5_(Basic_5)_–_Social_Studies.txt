Introductory Activities 2. Processes of Changing Culture and Differences or How Cultures can be Changed 3. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. state the way its effects changes in cultures and traditions. 2. make a list of different Nigerian languages. ENTRY BEHAVIOR The pupils can identify some of the cultural changes. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of map of Nigeria showing different languages. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book – Basic Social Studies for Primary Schools 4 with Security Education by E E Ezewu, J A Olawepo, C C Anadi, J A Adeyanju. All Relevant Materials Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1  – INTRODUCTION Culture is the way of life of a group of people. These cultures are vary from one community to another. A good culture might be a bad culture to another community. To united cultural diversity, there is need for change. Changes are not automatic but process. These take education through enlightenment, discussion to convince one another and dialogue to agree on issues. PROCESSES OF CHANGING CULTURE AND DIFFERENCES/HOW CULTURES CAN BE CHANGED? 1. EDUCATION Enlightening people in school, at home by telling tales by moonlight, workshops, seminars, training, etc. 2. DISCUSSION This is when people come together to sincerely speak their mind on the need to change a particular culture. 3. DIALOGUE This is coming together of people to agree on a certain issues. LESSON 2 – DIFFERENT LANGUAGES IN NIGERIA There are over two hundred and fifty different languages in Nigeria. Some of these languages are – 1. Yoruba 2. Igbo 3. Hausa 4. Efik 5. Kanuri 6. Idoma 7. Tiv 8. Fulani 9. Esan 10. Awori 11. Nupe 12. Ibibio 13. Urhobo 14. Boki 15. Baruba, etc. MAP OF NIGERIA SHOWING DIFFERENT PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Discusses with the pupils, the aspects of our cultures that are good and bad. Pupil’s Activities – Mention the aspects of good and bad cultures. 3. Ask pupils to make a list of different languages in Nigeria. Pupil’s Activities – List as many languages they know. 4. Lets each pupil to identify the location of their language on the map. Pupil’s Activities – Identify their language on the map. 5. Guides pupils to draw a map of Nigeria showing different languages. Pupil’s Activities – Draw a map of Nigeria showing different languages. 6. Summary the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
