LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should be able to: 1. differentiate between solid and non solid materials. 2. identify regular and irregular solids. 3. measure the volume of regular solids; 4. measure the volume of irregular solids. RATIONALE Solids are something strong or hard. There are two (2) of solid – regular and irregular solids. Measurement is the action of measuring something. Volume is the space that is enclosed by a vessel or occupied by a substance. The concept of measuring the volume of solid objects will enable the pupils to understand and appreciate how to measure regular and irregular solid. LEARNING MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Objects in the classroom e.g. cupboard, school box, books, etc. 2. Stone 3. Water 4. Metre rule 5. Tape 7. Rule 8. Beakers 9. Measuring cylinders. PREVIOUS KNOWLEDGE The pupils can identify solid objects.
