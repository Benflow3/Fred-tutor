LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to – 1. add and subtract numbers involving three or more items. 2. solve mixed operation problems involving addition and subtraction. RATIONALE LEARNING MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Flash cards. Abacus. Fraction chart. Cardboard.
