Introductory Activities 2. Recite the Lord’s Prayer 3. Things to Pray for PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – make a list of things to pray for. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR The Lord’s Prayer INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Chart showing things to pray for. Bible Reading – James 2:7 METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Asks the pupils to state things to pray for. Pupil’s Expected Response – Food, Money, Protection, etc. Teacher’s Activities – Summarize the pupil’s points as stated below – THINGS TO PRAYER FOR 1. Wisdom and understanding 2. Forgiveness 3. Provision 4. Discipline 5. Safety 6. Protection 7. Deliverance 8. Peace 9. Faith 10. Holy Spirit 11. Fruit of the Holy Spirit or Gift of the Holy Spirit PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Guides pupils to identify and state things to pray for. Pupil’s Activities – State things to pray for. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Power of Prayer – The Evidence of Power of Prayer (Primary 2) LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to: 1. recite the Lord’s Prayer 2. mention 5 things to pray for.
