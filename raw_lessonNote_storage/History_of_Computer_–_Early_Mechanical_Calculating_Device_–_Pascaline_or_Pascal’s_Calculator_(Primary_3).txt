EARLY MECHANICAL CALCULATING DEVICE – PASCALINE Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Displays Pascaline (if available) or a chart showing Pascaline for the pupils to observe and describe. PASCALINE Pascaline is mechanical calculator that perform addition and subtraction of two numbers directly, perform multiplication and division through repeated addition or subtraction. It is also known as Pascal’s calculator or arithmetic machine. PRACTICAL DEMONSTRATION USING PASCALINE FOR BASIC ARITHMETIC SUCH ADDITION AND SUBTRACTION Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – if the device is available, call for expert if you are unable to operate the device or watch YouTube video how to use the device. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Shows pictures of Pascaline computing device to the class. Pupil’s Activities – Bring pictures/cut outs of Pascaline computing devices. 3. Demonstrates the use of Pascaline. Pupil’s Activities – Use the Pascaline to solve different arithmetic problems. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
