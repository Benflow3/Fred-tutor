Introductory Activities 2. Review on Meaning of Prayer 3. Types of Prayer PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – state the types of prayer. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR The Lord’s Prayer INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Chart showing types of prayer METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Says what prayer is and ask the pupils to say what prayer is. Pupil’s Expected Response – Prayer is a way we talk to God and God talks to us. TYPES OF PRAYER 1. Pray of thanksgiving Act 4:23 2. Prayer of Confession of sins Luke 18:19 – 23 3. Prayer of petition 1 Samuel 1:17 4. Prayer of Intercessory Genesis 18: 2 – 23 Teacher’s Activities – Discuss different types of prayer with the pupils. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Discuss the types of prayer. Pupil’s Activities – State the types of prayer. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Things to Pray for (Primary 2) LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to: State 3 types of prayer.
