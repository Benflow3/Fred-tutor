LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: 1. find the prime, even and odd numbers 2. find common factors of 2-digit whole numbers 3. find the HCF of 2-digit whole numbers. 4. find common multiples of 2-digit whole numbers 5. find the LCM of 2-digit whole numbers. RATIONALE LEARNING MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of charts of factors and multiples of numbers. PREVIOUS KNOWLEDGE The pupils have the knowledge of multiplication of numbers up to 12 x 12.
