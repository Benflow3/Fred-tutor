CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Display some of the sample table tennis equipment for the pupils to identify and state its uses. Pupil’s Activities – This is a table tennis bat, ball and net. It is used for playing table tennis game. Teacher’s remark – Correct. Table tennis is one of the major sports in the world. Ask the pupils to describe how table tennis game is played. Summary the key points on the board. MEANING OF TABLE TENNIS Table tennis is one of the major sports in the world. It is played at all levels of competition. Both young and old people play the game but at world championships, it is a young person’s game. It is known as bat/racket game. It is a game of professional and amateur. It is played on a standard table, bench, any table, even on the floor with bat, broken slate or  hand. The game is played by a minimum two people in form of singles and maximum of four people in form of doubles or mixed doubles. The game takes place on a hard table divided by a net. LESSON 2 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Organize the pupils into two or groups, improvise table tennis (if the standard table not available) for the pupils play and practice some table tennis basic skills. Pupil’s Activities – Participate activity in the table tennis game. Teacher’s remark – Good job you. Just like in football game, there are basic skills required in table tennis game. Discuss the basic skills of tennis game as they practice and on the key points on the board. BASIC SKILLS/TECHNIQUES 1. The grip The bat (racket) should be regarded as an extension of the hand, therefore a correct and practical grip is of prime importance. The two different types of grips are: Shake hand grip (horizontal grip) Penholder grip (vertical grip) 2. Service The table tennis serve is the most important stroke in the game because it provides the only situation in which you have total control over how and where you play the ball. However, before you can master the correct table tennis serving technique, you’ll need to ensure that you’re using the correct table tennis grip and that you’ve mastered the four basic table tennis strokes. Pre – serve Planning to serve The toss and strike 3. Forehand It is a stroke used for rally or continuous play using the forehand side or face of the bat. 4. Backhand This stroke is used for rally or continuous playing using the backhand side or back of the bat. 5. Scoring A match is played best 3 of 5 games (or 4/7 or 5/9). For each game, the first player to reach 11 points wins that game, however a game must be won by at least a two point margin. A point is scored after each ball is put into play (not just when the server wins the point as in volleyball). The edges of the table are part of the legal table surface, but not the sides. ASSIGNMENT Draw a table tennis board. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Explains table tennis game and its basic skills; Pupil’s Activities – Demonstrates the skills in table tennis, practice the skills. 3. Summarizes the major points on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
