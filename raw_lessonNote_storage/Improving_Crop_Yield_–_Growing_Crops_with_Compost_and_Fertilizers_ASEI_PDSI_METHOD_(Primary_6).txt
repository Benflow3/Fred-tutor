Privacy Policy Home AI Plan Lesson Notes Improving Crop Yield – Growing Crops with Compost and Fertilizers ASEI PDSI METHOD (Primary 6) Improving Crop Yield – Growing Crops with Compost and Fertilizers ASEI PDSI METHOD (Primary 6) BASIC SCIENCE BASIC SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY SMASE – ASEI PDSI METHOD FIRST TERM WEEK 8 PRIMARY 6 TOPIC – GROWING CROPS LEARNING AREA Lesson One – Growing Crops with Compost and Fertilizer Lesson Two – Conditions for Better Crops Lesson Three – Importance of Manure and Fertilizers on the soil Revision and Test TIME – 40 Minutes each. LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: 1. name and describe some common fertilizers. 2. use compost and inorganic fertilizers to grow crops. 3. compare the effects of using compost and inorganic fertilizer inorganic fertilizer in growing crops. RATIONALE A crop is any useful plant intentionally grown for its useful value. Plants grown on a large scale are called crops. Growing crops include loosening the soil, planting, special watering, moving plants when they grow bigger, and harvesting, among others. To grow a better crop, there is need for a better seed planted a good and rich soil. A soil that is not fertile can be made fertile by adding fertilizer. The concept of growing crops with fertilizer will enable the pupils to identify different fertilizer used for growing crops and appreciate importance of fertilizer in growing better crops.
