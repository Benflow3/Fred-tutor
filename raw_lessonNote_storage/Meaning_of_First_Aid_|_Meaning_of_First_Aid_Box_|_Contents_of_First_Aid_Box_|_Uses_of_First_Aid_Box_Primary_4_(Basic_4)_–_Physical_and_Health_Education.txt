Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. describe first aid. 2. differentiate between first aid and first aid box. 3. list items inside first aid box. ENTRY BEHAVIOURS The pupils can identify some of the contents of first aid box. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: first aid box and its contents school’s first aid box charts pictures textbooks METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Display some of the items in a first aid box for the pupils to identify and discuss. Pupil’s Activities – This is bandage, scissor, cotton, etc. Teacher’s remark – Correct. The above items are contents of first aid box. Discuss the meaning of first aid and first aid box and summary the key points on the board. MEANING OF FIRST AID Many lives are saved through first aid, given before the emergency services arrived. First aid is the immediate care and support give to someone who is injured or slicked. First aid is given by a qualified person. MEANING OF FIRST AID BOX A first aid box is a collection of supplies and equipment that is used to give medical treatment. LESSON 2 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Ask the pupils state some of the items inside the first aid box. Pupil’s Activities – Bandage, scissor, iodine, etc. Teacher’s Activities – Correct. Comment and list the items inside the first aid box on the board. CONTENTS OF FIRST AID BOX (KIT) A basic first aid kit may contain: 1. First aid books 2. Bondages 3. Scissors 4. Cotton wool 5. Gentia violet 6. Guaze 7. Iodine 8. Plaster of different sizes 9. Thermometer 10. Safety pins 11. Hand towel 12. Antiseptic (Dettol) 13. Cleansing agent/soap 14. Splint, etc. ASSIGNMENT Find out the uses of first aid box. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Shows the contents of a First Aid Box to pupils and lets the pupils identify each of the contents of first aid box. Pupil’s Activities – Identify content of first aid box. 3. Discuss the meaning of first aid and first aid box. Pupil’s Activities – Listen to the teacher’s explanation. 4. Summary the key points on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
