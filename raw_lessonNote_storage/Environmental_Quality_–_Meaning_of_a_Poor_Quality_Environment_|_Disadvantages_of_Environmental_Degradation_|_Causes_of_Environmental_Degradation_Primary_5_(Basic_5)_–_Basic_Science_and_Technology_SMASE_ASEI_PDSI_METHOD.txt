LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: 1. explain the meaning of poor quality environment. 2. identify features of a poor environment. 3. identify features of a good environment 4. state the disadvantages of an unhealthy environment. RATIONALE The quality of an environment is determined by the characteristics of that environment. Some human activities such as oil spills, uncontrolled industrial works, over fishing, applying chemicals to soil, bush burning, playing loud music, dumping refuse indiscriminately, etc. have degraded the Nigerian environment. Poor quality environment is  the changing in the structure of the environment in undesirable ways. The concept of poor quality environment will enable pupils to understand and identify the features of poor quality environment and appreciate the importance of avoiding these features. LEARNING MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Potted plants Materials – brooms, rakes, hoes, cut lasses, machetes, baskets. Pictures of an attractive surrounding. PREVIOUS KNOWLEDGE A good or bad environment is characteristics by a certain futures. The pupils can identify the features good or bad of environmental quality.
