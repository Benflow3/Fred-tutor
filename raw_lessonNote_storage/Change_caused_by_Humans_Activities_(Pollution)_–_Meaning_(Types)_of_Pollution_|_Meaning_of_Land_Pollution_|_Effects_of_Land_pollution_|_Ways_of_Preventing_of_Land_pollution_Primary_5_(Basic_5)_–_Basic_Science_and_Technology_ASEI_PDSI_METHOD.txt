LEARNING OUTCOME By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: 1. explain the meaning of land pollution. 2. mention things that can pollute the land. RATIONALE Land pollution is the destruction of our land surfaces directly or indirectly as a result of man’s activities and use/misuse of land resources. Land pollution does not only make the land unsafe for living it destroys the beauty of our earth. Common causes of land pollution include dumping of refuse on the land, throwing chemicals on the soil, agricultural practices, mining, and discharge of industrial waste on land. Land pollution can have adverse effect on the health of animals and humans. The harmful chemicals that get into the soil and water can cause cancers, deformities, and skin problems. In this lesson, the pupils will be able to understand what is land pollution and its effects on living and non living thing and how to reduce and prevent land pollution in the future. LEARNING M ATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of the school compound. PREVIOUS KNOWLEDGE The pupils can different between clean and unclean environments. The unclean environments are characterized with different waste that are dangerous and harmful man and animals. These harmful substances introduced into the environment are called pollutants. The types of pollution we have are land, water, air and noise pollution. MEANING OF POLLUTION Land pollution is the release unwanted stuff, waste materials and harmful substances on the land. Polluted land is unsafe for human, plants and animals. CAUSES OF LAND POLLUTION Land pollution can happen through human activities like – 1. Dumping of refuse 2. Deforestation 3. Bush burning 4. Soil erosion 5. Agricultural activities 6.
