Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. identify named objects at home, on the way to and from school, and in the school compound. 2. clearly pronounce names of objects. 3. state the uses of some of the objects. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR The pupils can identify some of the objects at home and in the school. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of Pictures, Charts, Real objects, Brightly coloured picture books. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. This is a fan, etc. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Displays samples of some common objects within and around us. 3. Lets pupils identify each of the object by name. Pupil’s Activities – Identify common objects within and around. 4. Helps pupils to identify some of the objects in the classroom and outside the classroom. 3. Associates colours with objects in the classroom. 4. Guides pupils to identify objects of named colours. Pupil’s Activities – Identify objects with colour in the classroom. 5. Helps pupils to state some of the uses of common objects within and around us. Pupil’s Activities – State the uses of common objects. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
