Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – 1. state the meaning of computer; 2. list uses of computer. ENTRY BEHAVIOR The pupils are required to already have learned… INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Monitor CPU Keyboard UPS Storing devices METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher Activities – Display functional computer system (if available). If not, display a chart showing computer for the pupils to identify and state its uses. Pupil’s Activities – This is a computer. It is used for…. Teacher’s remark – You have done a great job. This is a computer. A Computer is an electronic device that allow people to perform different task such as typing letter, graphic design, computing, playing game, send and receiving mails, communication, online shopping and banking and so on. Discuss the meaning of computer. Note the meaning on the board. MEANING OF COMPUTER A computer as an electronic machine that accepts data as input, processes the data and gives out information as output. HAND ON ACTIVITIES Allow the pupils to have fun by playing games, listen to music or watching movies if available. ASSIGNMENT Find out the uses of computer system. LESSON 2 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Ask the pupils, what is a computer? Pupil’s Activities – A computer is….. Teacher’s remark – Give a cheer for defining a computer. Comment and display chart showing pupils learning with computer and other uses for them to identify and state. Summary the uses of computer on the board. USES OF COMPUTER Computers are used by different people in an organizations for different purposes: 1. Education it is used for learning, teaching and research. 2. Government It is used for planning and budgeting. 3. Healthcare It used for recording, running test and taking care of patients. 4. Business It is used for record keeping, analysing and interpreting information. 5. Entertainment and recreation It is for playing music, watching movie, also for playing games. 6. Defence It is used for tactic and defending a territory. 7. Buying and selling It is for shopping and selling items online. 8. Arts It is used for drawing and painting. 9. Recording information It is used for reporting an event such as wedding ceremony, birthday party, graduation ceremony, etc. 10. Safety It is used for control traffic and give signal on the condition of the road. 11. Weather stations It is used for recording and reporting the present conditions the atmosphere. 12. Sports It is used in broadcasting sport’s activities. 13. Manufacturing It is used for design, production, marketing and advertisement. 14. Banking ASSIGNMENT Find out the advantages of using computer. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Guides in discussing the meaning of Computer; Pupil’s Activities – Participate in discussing meaning of computer. 3. List uses and areas where computer are used on daily basis; Pupil’s Activities – state the uses of computer and areas where it is used. 4. Guides pupils to use the computer to play games, type text, watch educational films, etc. Pupil’s Activities – Text, draw, and play games using computer. 5. Summarize the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
