Introductory Activities 2. Care of the Bathroom and Toilet 3. Practical Cleaning of the Bathroom and Toilet 4. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. Identify various rooms in a house. 2. State how to keep the toilets clean daily. 3. Carry out daily cleaning of the house. ENTRY BEHAVIOURS The pupils can identify and state the tools and equipments used for cleaning various rooms in a house or building. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Pictures of various functional rooms in a house. 2. A chart of different steps involved in a daily cleaning of various functional rooms in a house. 3. Brooms, ceiling brooms, duster, dust pan, refuse bin, flower in a small vase. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTION A room is one of the separate parts of the inside of a house. Different rooms have different facilities based on their Uses. There are many rooms in a house which serves different purpose – 1. Sitting room 2. Bedroom 3. Dining room 4. Kitchen 5. Food store 6. Bathroom 7. Toilet BATHROOM A bathroom is a room in a house where people take their bath. Sometimes, it is called restroom. Some bathroom contains toilet facility. CARE FOR THE BATHROOM Caring for bathroom involves – 1. Daily and weekly cleaning 2. Sweeping 3. Airing 4. Scrubbing 5. Using disinfectant 6. Flush immediately after dedicate and urinate LESSON 2 – TOILET AS A FUNCTIONAL ROOM A toilet is a room in a house where people defecate and urinate. CARE FOR THE TOILETS 1. Airing of the toilets 3. Removing cobwebs 3. Sweeping 4. Dusting 5. Washing 6. Disinfectant Some bathroom and toilet serve as a single room while in some cases, they are separated. LESSON 3 – PRACTICAL CLEANING OF SCHOOL TOILETS Organize the class into two or more groups for each group to clean the school toilets. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Explains the steps involved in the daily care of toilet in a house. Pupil’s Activities – State the steps involved in the daily care of toilet in a house. 3. Demonstrates daily cleaning of bathroom and toilet. Pupil’s Activities – Participate in daily and weekly cleaning of bathroom and toilet. 4. Summarize the lesson on the board. Copy short notes on daily cleaning of rooms.
