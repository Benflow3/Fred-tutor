Introduction 2.  Sense of Smell 3. Importance of Nose 4. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. identify nose as sense of smell 2. identify food, fruit, fish, etc. through sense of smell. 3. detect pleasant and unpleasant smell. 4. classify smell as pleasant and unpleasant smell. 5. appraise the importance of nose in their daily activities. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR The pupils can differentiate between pleasant and unpleasant smells. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of fruit, bean, rice, fresh fish, etc. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTION (DETECTING VARIOUS ODOURS) Teacher organizes pupils in groups or pairs depending on the size of the class to close their eyes in order to detect various odours of, 1. Rice 2. Beans 3. Bread 4. Orange 5. Tea, etc. SENSE OF SMELL (NOSE) Nose is part of our respiratory that help you breathe in and out. It is one of the sense of organs that enables us to smell. IMPORTANCE OF NOSE The importance of nose are as follows: 1. It allows air to enter our body. 2. It provide us with sense of smell. 3. It filters and cleans air you breathe. 4. It warms and moistens air 5. It enables us to detect pleasant and unpleasant objects. LESSON 2 – DETECTING PLEASANT AND UNPLEASANT SMELL Based on the existing groups or pairs, guides pupils to state, 1. 10 pleasant food or substance. 2. 10 unpleasant food or substance. LESSON 3 – REVISION AND WEEKLY ASSESSMENT (TEST) PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Teacher organizes pupils in groups or pairs depending on the size of the class. 3. Teacher displays different food or substance in a close container. 4. Teacher call a representative from each group or pair to detect the object by smelling. Pupil’s Activities – Identify food substance by smell. 5. Teacher uses the food substance to introduce the lesson and leads a discussion on the meaning of sense of smell and its importance. Pupil’s Activities – Pay attention to the lesson introduction to understand the concept of the lesson. 7. Teacher guides to differentiate between pleasant and unpleasant substance with appropriate examples. Pupil’s Activities – Describe pleasant and unpleasant smell. 8. Teacher summarizes the lessons on the board with appropriate lesson evaluation. Pupil’s Activities – Participate actively in the summary of the lesson by responding to the questions and write as instructed. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – The Sense of Taste – The Tongue Primary 2 (Basic 2) Term 3 Week 6 Basic Science and Technology LESSON EVALUATION Teacher asks pupils to, 1. identify nose as sense of smell 2. identify food, fruit, fish, etc. through sense of smell. 3. detect pleasant and unpleasant smell. 4. classify smell as pleasant and unpleasant smell. 5. appraise the importance of nose in their daily activities. 6. associate odours with specific pleasant and unpleasant smell.
