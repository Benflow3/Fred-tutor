Introductory Activities 2. Materials used for cleaning the Body PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. list the materials used for cleaning the body. 2. list the materials used for cleaning the body. 3. demonstrate the use of the different body care materials. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR The pupils can identify some of the materials used for the care of the body and demonstrate how to use them. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Illustrative chart or picture of body care. 2. Body care materials such as towels, brush, sponge, comb, body cream, etc. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTION MATERIALS USED FOR THE CARE OF THE BODY 1. Soap 2. Sponge 3. Water 4. Hair brush 5. Body and hair creams 6. Towel 7. Face powder, etc. LESSON 2 – DEMONSTRATE HOW TO USE BODY CARE MATERIALS PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Displays sample of some of the body care materials. 3. Lets pupils examine each of the material. 4. Asks pupils to mention the name each of the materials displayed. Pupil’s Activities – Identify body care materials on display. Describe the uses of each materials identified. 5. Summarizes the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Write as instructed. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Care of Each Part of the Body (Primary 1) LESSON EVALUATION Ask pupils to – 1. identify the different body parts care materials. 2. state 5 materials used for care of the body. 3. demonstrate the use of the different body care materials.
