Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to state the uses of first aid box. ENTRY BEHAVIOURS The pupils can identify the contents of first aid box. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: first aid box and its contents school’s first aid box charts pictures textbooks METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Based the previous assignment, ask the pupils to state the uses of first aid box. Pupil’s Activities – First aid box is used…. Teacher’s remark – Listen to the pupils, Comment, discuss and summary some of the uses of first aid box on the board USES OF FIRST AID BOX A first aid box is a collection of supplies and equipment that is used to give immediate treatment. These collections served the following purpose: 1. It is used for giving immediate care and support to patients. 2. It is used for reducing infections. 3. It is used to save live. 4. It is used for reducing the time of recovery. 5. It is used for reducing pain. 6. It is used for preventing/reducing excessive blood loss. 7. It used for preventing the condition of the patients from getting from worse. LESSON 2 – USES OF EACH ITEM INSIDE FIRST AID BOX Guide the pupils to itemised all the contents of first aid, state the uses of each item. Summary the key points on the board. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Discuss the uses of first aid box. Pupil’s Activities – Participate actively in the class discussion. 3. Guides and lets the pupils state the uses of each materials in first box. Pupil’s Activities – State the uses of first aid materials. 4. Summary the key points on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher write. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. NEXT LESSON – First Term Examination – work in progress… EVALUATION Ask pupils to: 1. describe first aid. 2. differentiate between first aid and first aid box. 3. state 3 uses of first first aid box.
