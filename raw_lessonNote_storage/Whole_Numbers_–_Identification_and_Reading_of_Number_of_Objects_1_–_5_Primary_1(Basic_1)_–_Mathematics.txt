For example, colour 2 triangles. LESSON 3 – REVISION ON LESSON 1 LESSON 4 – REVISION ON LESSON 2 PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Displays sample of some objects. 3. Lets pupils identify each object. Pupil’s Activities – Identify each of the object displayed. 4. Displays the mixture of one pencil, 2 rulers, 2 books, 4 pens and 5 keys. 5. Lets pupils group the objects in their like term. 6. Ask them to count the number of objects in each group. Pupil’s Activities – Count the number of objects in each group. 3. Asks pupils to read the number of objects aloud. Pupil’s Activities – Read the number of objects. 4. Guides pupils to colour a certain number of objects. Pupil’s Activities – Colour a certain number of objects. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
