(Primary 3) TOPIC – CARE OF ROOMS IN THE HOUSE LEARNING AREA 1. Introductory Activities 2. Materials for Home Cleaning 3. Cleaning Agents PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – 1. identify materials for cleaning. 2. state the uses each material. 3. identify and state home made cleaning agents. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Cleaning Materials – Brooms, brush, etc. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Chart showing different parts of rooms in the house and room’s cleaning materials METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s Activities – Displays samples or chart showing different materials used for house care for the pupils to identify and state the uses of each material. Pupil’s Activities – Identify and state the uses of different articles for house cleaning. Teacher’s remark – Listen to the pupils and give a cheer for quick respond to questions and class participation. Note their points on the board. ARTICLES FOR CLEANING ROOMS 1. Grind egg shells 2. Grind snail shells 3. Salt 4. Sand 5. Vim (scouring powder) 6. Detergent 7. Bar soap 8. Wood ash 9. Candle wax 10 Kerosene 11. Air freshener (optional) 12. Duster 13. Broom 14. Brushes 15. Long brushes and brooms 16. Native chalk 17. Empty bottles CLEANING AGENTS 1. Home made Vim 2. Salt 3. Fine sand 4. Ash 5. Grinded sail shell or eggshell, etc. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Lists materials for cleaning: broom (long and short broom), brushes, mop, cloth/dusters. Pupil’s Activities – Bring raw materials for making cleaning agents. 3. Discusses steps for cleaning rooms. Pupil’s Activities – Participate in the class discussion. 4. Demonstrates the steps for cleaning a room. Pupil’s Activities – Clean their class rooms. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Steps in Cleaning Sitting Rooms and Produce Home Cleaning Agents (Primary 3) LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to: 1. mention 10 materials for cleaning rooms. 2. state 5 cleaning agents.
