ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Cup, table, orange, etc. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Real objects 2. Pictures 3. Toys. METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1.
