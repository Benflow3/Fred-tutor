Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. identify mainframe computer. 2. describe the size, speed and uses of mainframe computer. ENTRY BEHAVIOURS The pupils can identify computer by purpose. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of chart shows mainframe computer. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – MAINFRAME COMPUTER Mainframe computers are second largest and powerful computer by size. SIZE OF MAINFRAME COMPUTER A mainframe computer’s size depends primarily on its age. The modern mainframe is considerably smaller – often approximately the size of a large refrigerator. SPEED OF MAINFRAME COMPUTER The speed of large computers can be 70-100 mips of more. LESSON 2 – USES OF MAINFRAME COMPUTER Mainframe computers are large-scale computer systems. They are designed to process and store very large amounts of information (data) that smaller systems such as PCs cannot handle. It can support hundreds and often thousands of users and computer professionals. They are used by government agencies such as the Internal Revenue Service, Electoral Commission and Census commission, vote computations, individual tax records, payroll, etc. Small and large retail companies use mainframes to register sales, update stock and manage inventory levels. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Display chart showing mainframe computer. Pupil’s Activities – Identify and describe the physical part of mainframe computer. 3. Discusses the size, speed and uses of mainframe computer. Pupil’s Activities – Identify the size, speed and uses of mainframe computer. 4. Summarize the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the pupils write. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
