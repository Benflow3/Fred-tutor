(Primary 5) CHRISTIAN RELIGIOUS STUDIES RELIGION AND NATIONAL VALUES FIRST TERM WEEK 10 PRIMARY 5 THEME – GOD PLAN FOR US PREVIOUS LESSON – TYPES OF WORK TOPIC – REASONS WHY WE SHOULD WORK? PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – explain why people should work. explain the importance of every occupation. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Bible. Working implements e.g.. hoe, fishing net, wood, etc. METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information Share and like our Facebook page or Twitter . CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTION Reading Bible 28 blessed them, and said, “Have many children, so that your descendants will live all over the earth and bring it under their control. I am putting you in charge of the fish, the birds, and all the wild animals. 29 I have provided all kinds of grain and all kinds of fruit for you to eat; 30 but for all the wild animals and for all the birds I have provided grass and leafy plants for food”—and it was done. WHY WE SHOULD WORK God wants us to work. We work – To earn living. To take care of our needs. To provide for your family. So you don’t become lazy and a busybody. To give to the church. To give to others. To thank God. (GENERAL 1:28 – 30;3:17; 2THESSALONIANS 3:3 – 13). PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; Discusses the meaning of work with the pupils. Pupil’s Activities – Explain the term, “work”. Lists different types of work. Pupil’s Activities – State types of work. Gives reasons why we should work – We supply our needs through hard work. Pupil’s Activities – Give reasons why we should work CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Revision, Test and Examination LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to list 5 reasons why they should work.
