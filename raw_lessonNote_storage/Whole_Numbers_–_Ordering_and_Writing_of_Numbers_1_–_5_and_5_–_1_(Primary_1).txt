Counting Numbers 1 – 5 2. Writing Numbers 1 – 5 and 5 – 1 3. Arranging Numbers 1 – 5 and 5 – 1 in Order of their Magnitudes (quantities) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – 1. count correctly up to 5. 2. write correctly numbers 1 – 5. 3. arrange numbers 1 – 5 in order of their magnitudes (quantities). ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Counting and Writing of Numbers 1 – 5 INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of counters – stones, beans, bottle tops, buttons, leaves, nylon bags, number cards, etc. METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information – Ordering and Writing of Numbers from 1 – 5 ASEI PDSI METHOD (Primary 1) CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON ONE – ORDERING AND WRITING NUMBERS 1 – 5 INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Asks the pupils to match the following objects with their corresponding flash card number 1 – 5. ⚽⚽⚽⚽                             5 ⚽⚽                                       3 ⚽                                            2 ⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽                        4 ⚽⚽⚽                                  1 ARRANGE AND READ Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Asks to arrange the objects according from 1 – 5. ⚽                                          1 ⚽⚽                                      2 ⚽⚽⚽                                 3 ⚽⚽⚽⚽                             4 ⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽                        5 Read aloud – 1 2 3 4 5 LESSON EVALUATION/ASSIGNMENT Write Number 1 – 5. LESSON TWO – ORDERING AND WRITING NUMBERS 5 – 1 ARRANGE AND READ Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Asks to arrange the objects according from 5 – 1. ⚽                                          1 ⚽⚽                                      2 ⚽⚽⚽                                 3 ⚽⚽⚽⚽                             4 ⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽                        5 Read aloud – 5 4 3 2 1 LESSON EVALUATION/ASSIGNMENT Arrange the following Numbers – 4 2 5 1 3 as follows – A. 1 – 5 B. 5 – 1 Note – Repeat the above activities in other lessons. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Use counters to arrange objects in magnitude or in ordering form. Pupil’s Activities – Use counter to arrange objects in magnitude or in ordering form. 3. Reads number 1 – 5. Pupil’s Activities – Read the numbers 1 – 5. Write the number 1 – 5 in exercise book. 4. Lesson evaluation. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
