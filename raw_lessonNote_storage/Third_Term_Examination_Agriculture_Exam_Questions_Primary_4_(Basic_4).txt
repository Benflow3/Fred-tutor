These are farm tools except ______. (a) cutlass (b) hoe (c) needle 2. ______ is a plant growing where it is not needed. (a) Flower (b) Weed (c) Crops 3. Fishing nets is used to catch ______. (a) fish (b) cat (c) flies 4. ______ is used for keeping poultry birds. (a) Bamboo (b) Battery cage (c) Wire 5. ______ is a simple farm tools. (a) Cutlass (b) Hope (c) All listed 6. Rake is used for ______. (a) cutting (b) digging (c) gathering dirts 7. Another name for tilling is ______. (a) wetting (b) ploughing (c) sprinkling 8. ______ is a tool for tilling (a) Plough (b) Shears (c) Scissors 9. Tillage is done to ______ the Soil. (a) wash (b) loosen (c) wash 10. ______ is a digging tool. (a) Bag (b) Cup (c) Shovel 11. Spade is a ______ tool. (a) washing (b) cutting (c) digging 12. which of these is a transportation tool on the farm? (a) Car (b) Wheelbarrow (c) Lorry 13. ______ tools are used for carrying materials from one place to another. (a) Cutting (b) Transporting (c) Digging 14. Gathering tools are used to ______ materials or object. (a) sleep (b) gather (c) plant 15. Gathering tools are made of ______. (a) paper (b) iron (c) rubber SECTION A – THEORY INSTRUCTION – Attempt all questions in this section. 1. Define simple farm tools. 2. Mention 5 simple farm tools. 3. What is tillage? 4. List out 3 tilling tools. 5. What is digging? 6. Mention 3 digging tools. 7. Write out 2 gathering tools. 8. Write the uses of cutlass and  rake.
