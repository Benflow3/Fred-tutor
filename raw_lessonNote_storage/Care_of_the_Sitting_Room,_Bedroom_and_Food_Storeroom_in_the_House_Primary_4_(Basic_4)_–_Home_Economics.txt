Introductory Activities 2. Care of Sitting Room and Bedrooms 3. Care of Foodstuff Room 4. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. Identify various rooms in a house. 2. State how to keep the house clean daily. 3. Attempt 70% (and above) assessment test correctly. ENTRY BEHAVIOURS The pupils can identify and state the tools and equipments used for cleaning various rooms in a house or building. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Pictures of various functional rooms in a house. 2. A chart of different steps involved in a daily cleaning of various functional rooms in a house. 3. Brooms, ceiling brooms, duster, dust pan, refuse bin, flower in a small vase. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTION A room is one of the separate parts of the inside of a house. Different rooms have different facilities based on their Uses. There are many rooms in a house which serves different purpose – 1. Sitting room 2. Bedroom 3. Dining room 4. Kitchen 5. Food store 6. Bathroom 7. Toilet SITTING ROOM A sitting room is a room in a house where people are welcome, sit and relax. It is part of the living room with chairs and center table, electronics. BEDROOM A bedroom is a room in a house with a bed and it mainly used for resting and sleeping. Each of the bedroom has its bathroom/toilet. Some bedrooms shared bathroom/toilet except for the master bedroom. CARE FOR SITTING ROOM AND BEDROOM Caring for sitting room involves – 1. Daily cleaning 2. Airing 3. Sweeping 4. Dusting LESSON 2 – FOODSTUFF ROOM FOODSTUFF ROOM A food store is a room in a house where food are stored. CARE FOR THE STOREROOM Caring for sitting involves – 1. Weekly cleaning 2. Airing 3. Removal of cobwebs 4. Sweeping 5. Dusting 6. Arranging the food stuff LESSON 3 – CARING FOR SITTING ROOM, BEDROOM AND STOREROOM Reorganize the class as sitting room and foodstuff room for the pupils to demonstrate how to care for the sitting room and foodstuff room. Use the school toilet as bathroom. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Explains the steps involved in the daily care of sitting ROOM, bedrooms and foodstuff room in a house. Pupil’s Activities – Practice in groups how to remove cobwebs, sweep, dust and arrange furniture in the rooms. 3. Demonstrates daily cleaning of sitting rooms, food storeroom and bathroom. Pupil’s Activities – Participate in daily and weekly cleaning of different rooms. 4. Summarize the lesson on the board. Copy short notes on daily cleaning of rooms. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. NEXT LESSON LESSON EVALUATION Ask pupils to: 1. State 3 ways of caring for the sitting room. 2. Mention 3 ways of caring for the bedroom. 3. List 3 ways of caring for the foodstuff room.
