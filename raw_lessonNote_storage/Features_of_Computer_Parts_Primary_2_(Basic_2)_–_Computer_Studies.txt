Features of Computer Parts PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – identify the features of computer parts ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Parts of a Computer INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Computer system 2. Chart 3. Pictures. METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Displays a functional computer or chart showing a computer for the pupils to identify and describe. MEANING OF COMPUTER A computer is a machine that counts, calculates and draws. Computer thinks and calculates faster than you. Television looks like a computer monitor but it is not a computer monitor. FEATURES OF COMPUTER PARTS Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Touches each part of a computer for the pupils to identify by name. 1. Monitor 2. Keyboard 3. Mouse 4. Speakers 5. System unit PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Asks pupils to identify features of major computer parts e.g monitor, system unit, keyboard, mouse, etc. Pupil’s Activities – Touch and identify major parts of a computer such as Monitor, system unit, keyboard, etc. 3. Guides pupils to draw a computer showing major parts. Pupil’s Activities – Draw a computer showing major parts. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
