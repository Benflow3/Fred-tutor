Instruments for Measuring Length 2. Unit of Measuring Length 3. Measuring Length PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor) and should be able to – 1. name the instruments for measuring length. 2. measure and record the length, breadth, and height of objects. 3. state the metric units for measuring length. ENTRY BEHAVIOUR Tape rule, ruler, etc. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Tape rule 2. Meter rule 3. Objects in the classroom 4. The pupil’s height 5. A chart on standard unit of length METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information – Primary 3 – Scheme of Work and Plan Lesson Notes (All Subjects) Basic Science and Technology (Primary 3) CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON ONE – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Displays different instruments for measuring length for pupils to identify and ask them to state their uses. Teacher’s Activities – Note the pupils points on the board or cardboard. INSTRUMENTS FOR MEASURING LENGTH 1. Rule 3. Tape rule 3. Measuring Tape TRADITIONAL MEASURING INSTRUMENTS Teacher’s Pupil’s Activities – Asks pupils to mention other things we can use for measuring lengths of the above mentioned are not available. 1. Hand span 2. Foot step 3. Arm length 4. Rope 5. Straight plank, rode, etc. USES OF INSTRUMENTS FOR MEASURING LENGTHS Pupil’s Activities – They are used for measurement. LESSON EVALUATION/HOME WORK 1. Find out 5 things we measured with ruler, measuring tape or tape rule. 2. Find out 5 people that used ruler, measuring tape or tape rule. LESSON TWO – UNITS OF MEASURING LENGTH AND MEASURING OF LENGTH OF OBJECTS Teacher’s/Pupil’s – Asks pupils to examine measuring instruments – ruler, measuring tape and tape rule and tell you the alphabetical letter on the measuring rules. Records their observations. UNITS OF MEASURING LENGTH 1. Centimetre – cm 2. Millimeter – mm MEASURING OF LENGTH Teacher’s/Pupil’s Activities – Measuring the length of 1. Notebook – Length 1 ____________ and Length 2 ____________ 2. Textbook – Length 1 ____________ and Length 2 ____________ 3. Desk – Length 1 ____________, Length 2 ____________ and Length 3 ____________ 4. Their height – 5. Classroom – Length 1 ____________ and Length 2 ____________ MEANING OF LENGTH Pupil’s Activities – What is Length? Length is the distance between two points. LESSON EVALUATION/ASSIGNMENT Measure the length, breadth, and height of the following objects – 1. Cube 2. Cuboid WORKBOOK – WEEKLY ASSESSMENT First Term Workbook – Weekly Assessment for Basic Science and Technology (Primary 3) PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Uses a chart showing metric rule and tape rule to teach pupils the metric units of length (mm, cm). Pupil’s Activities – Read and learn the metric unit of length. 3. Shows pupils the correct ways of measuring length, breadth and height of objects including their heights. Pupil’s Activities – Measure the length, breadth and height of tables, desks, books in the classroom. Working in pairs, measure their heights. Working in pairs, measure their heights. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Measurement of Length and Mass – Mass (Primary 3) LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to: 1. name two instrument for measuring length. 2. state the metric units of length. 3. measures correctly the length, breadth and height of objects in the classroom. 4. record measurements correctly including units.
