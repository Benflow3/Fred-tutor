INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: Pictures Real Arts Pencils Crayons Drawing Books METHOD OF TEACHING – Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. Related Posts CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTION MEANING OF FUNCTIONAL ARTS A functional art is an artwork that is useful. Functional artworks are very beautiful and attractive. It is used mostly in the home. TYPES OF FUNCTIONAL ART WORKS WOVEN ITEMS Woven items are items made with raffia, straw, cotton and palm leaves. They are most at home. Mats Caps Beads Baskets Hand fans Clothing Cane chair Cardigan. CARVINGS Carving items are made from wood. Walking stick Mask Pestle and mortar Calabash Drum PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; Explains what functional art is. Pupil’s Activities – Explain the meaning of functional art. Shows illustrations of different types of functional art works. Pupil’s Activities – Identify different types of functional art works. Pupil’s Activities – Draw diagrams and states the uses of functional artworks in the home. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. Next Lesson – Singing LESSON EVALUATION Pupils to: identify functional art works used in the home. illustrate a functional art work. apply one colour on the object drawn. state the uses of different functional artwork.
