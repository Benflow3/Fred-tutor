INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of playground, balls, etc. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON INTRODUCTION Display chart showing children kicking, bouncing and throwing balls. BIG BALL To throw an object like ball, grip the ball firmly with two (2) hands if it is a big ball and take a step backward and a step forward as you released. Extend your arm sharply as the body is pushed outwards. SMALL BALL To throw small ball, grip the ball firmly with one hand and release. Extend your arm sharply as the body is pushed outwards. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson . Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Displays chart showing children kicking, bouncing and throwing balls. 3. Allows pupils to identify and describe each activity on the chart. Pupil’s Activities – Describe each of the activity on the chart. 4. Brings out a ball and throw it light to one of the pupils. 5. Identify them to identify an activity that presents throwing of ball. Pupil’s Activities – Watch the teacher and identify an activity that show throwing. 6. Guides pupils to grasp, pick up small object with one hand, and release it. Pupil’s Activities – Grasp, pick up small object with one hand, and release it. 3. Demonstrates the basic manipulative movements – throwing, supervise the children and offers assistance. Pupil’s Activities – Practice manipulative patterns of – throwing. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
