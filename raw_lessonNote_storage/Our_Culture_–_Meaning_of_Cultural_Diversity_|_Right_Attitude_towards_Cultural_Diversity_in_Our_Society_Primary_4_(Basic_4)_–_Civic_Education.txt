Introductory Activities 2. Meaning of Cultural Diversity 3. Right Attitude towards Cultural Diversity in Our Society 4. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. explain the word: culture and diversity. 2. explain cultural diversity. 3. imbibe right attitude to cultural diversity. ENTRY BEHAVIOR The pupils can define culture and give appropriate examples. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of real cultural materials and objects. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book – Basic Social Studies for Primary Schools 4 with Security Education by E E Ezewu, J A Olawepo, C C Anadi, J A Adeyanju. All Relevant Materials Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1  – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES MEANING OF CULTURE Culture means ways of life. MEANING OF DIVERSITY Diversity means difference. MEANING OF CULTURAL DIVERSITY Cultural Diversity means different people with different origins, religions and traditions living and interacting with one another. Cultural diversity has increased, exposing kids to new tastes and experiences. EXAMPLES OF CULTURAL FOODS IN NIGERIA Amala, Tuwo Shinkafa, Abasha, Efo riro, Oha, Masa, Okpa, Gbediri, Kilishi, Agbugbu, Ewedu, Fura da Nono and Ukwa. WORKING EXAMPLES Identify Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa food. ASSIGNMENT Find out the mode of greetings and dressing of – 1. Yoruba 2. Igbo 3. Hausa LESSON 2 – RIGHT ATTITUDES TOWARDS CULTURAL DIVERSITY There are different people living together as one in our society. These people have different backgrounds, culture and religion. To live in peace and unit, 1. we must respect other people. 2. we must accept individual differences. 3. we must appreciate cultural differences. 4. we must be honest with one another. 5. we must tolerate one another. 6. we must not argue about what you don’t know. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Display sample of real objects. 3. Asks pupils to identify and mention the culture or tribe each of the objects represent. Pupil’s Activities – Identify and mention objects of culture that belongs to the different groups in our society. For example, Lagos. 4. Discuss the right attitudes toward cultural diversity. Pupil’s Activities – Participate actively in the class discussion. 5. Helps pupils to show or manifest cultural differences; Pupil’s Activities – Demonstrate respect and tolerance to cultural differences. 6. Summary the key points on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. A. Cultural difference B. The same culture C. Good culture 4. Accepting one another as one Nigeria makes us ______. A. fighting one another B. hating one another C. eating one another food 5. ______ is not right towards cultural difference. A. Hatred B. Respect C.
