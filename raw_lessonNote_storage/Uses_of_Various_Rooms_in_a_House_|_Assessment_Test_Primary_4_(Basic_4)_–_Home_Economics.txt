Introductory Activities 2. Uses of various rooms in a house 3. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. identify various rooms in a house. 2. identify the uses of various rooms in a house. 3. Attempt 70% (and above) assessment test correctly. ENTRY BEHAVIOURS The pupils can identify and state the uses of various rooms in a house or building. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Pictures of various functional rooms in a house. 2. Pictures of uses of various rooms in a house. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTION A room is a space occupied in a house. A room is one of the separate parts of the inside of a house. Different rooms have different facilities based on their uses. There are many rooms in a house which serves different purpose. 1. Sitting room 2. Bedroom 3. Dining room 4. Kitchen 5. Food store 6. Bathroom 7. Toilet USES OF SITTING ROOM AND BEDROOM USES OF SITTING ROOM 1. It is used for welcoming our guests. 2. It is used for entertainment. 3. It is used for relaxation. USES OF BEDROOM 1. It is used for resting. 2. It is used for resting. 3. It is used for dressing up. 3. It is used for keeping and safeguarding our personal belongings. USES OF FOOD STORE 1. It is used for keeping our food items. 2. It is used to keeping pests and Insects away from our foodstuffs. 3. It is used for keeping our foodstuffs from food diseases. 4. It is used for keeping our kitchen tools and equipments. LESSON 2 – USES OF VARIOUS FUNCTIONAL ROOMS IN A HOUSE Continuation….. USES OF BEDROOM 1. It is used for our bathing. 2. It is used used for urinating. 3. It is used for washing dirty parts of body. 4. It is used for brushing our tooth. 5. It is used for washing our clothes. USES OF TOILET 1. It is used for defecating. 2. It is used for urinating. USES OF KITCHEN 1. It is used for cooking our foods. 2. It is cleaning our kitchen utensils. 3. It is used for serving food. USED OF DINING ROOM 1. It is used for eating our meals. 2. It is used for entertaining our guests. PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Presents pictures or charts of various rooms in a house. Pupil’s Activities – Identify the various rooms in a house with the help of a teacher. 3.Presents another picture showing various uses of the room. Pupil’s Activities – Give examples using their own homes. 4. Summary the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
