Primary 5 (Basic 5) – Civic Education CIVIC EDUCATION RELIGION AND NATIONAL VALUES FIRST TERM WEEK 1 PRIMARY 5 THEME: OUR VALUES PREVIOUS LESSON – Third Term Examination Civic Education Exam Questions Primary 4 (Basic 4) TOPIC – WHY MANY PEOPLE DO NOT OWN HOUSE LEARNING AREA 1. Introductory Activities 2. Meaning of House 3. Types of Houses 4. Importance of Owning Houses 5. Why many People do not Own House 6. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most of the pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. explain the meaning of house. 2. state types of houses. 3. mention the importance of owning a house. 4. state reasons why many people do not own houses in Nigeria. 5. attempt 70% (and above) of weekly assessment correctly. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. Pictures of houses 2. Resource persons in the town or villages. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTION MEANING OF HOUSE A house is a building in which people live. Or house is a place built to live in as family. Different houses are built for different purposes. For example, it is built for shelters, schools, hospitals, offices, etc. Houses are built with different materials. For example – houses built with blocks, wood, iron, etc. by people, cave by early man and animals, nest or den by animals, cage built for animals, etc. Some are simple and small while other are big and magnificent. TYPES OF HOUSES There are different types of houses in our community. Different house with different features. These features defined the types of houses – 1. Single rooms 2. Duplex 3. Mansion 4. Traditional house 5. Detached house 6. Semi – detached house 7. Terrace house 8. Pen house 9. Bungalow 10. Primitive house – for example – caves, mud houses, hut and tents. PARTS OF HOUSE Examples of the parts of a house are – 1. Bedroom 2. Living room or sitting room 3. Kitchen 4. Pantry 5. Store room 6. Toilet 7. Bathroom LESSON 2 – IMPORTANCE OF OWNING A HOUSE People owns a house for different reasons. For example, 1. For safety or security 2. For investments 3. For businesses 4. For storage 5. For manufacturing 6. For study, research and training 7. For medical purposes 8. For educational purposes 9. For relaxation 8. For sports and games, etc. WHY MANY PEOPLE DO NOT OWN HOUSES The reasons why people do not own houses including – 1. Poverty 2. Cost of building materials 3. Difficulty of obtaining loans 4. Problems of land allocation 5. Unemployment 6. Government policies 7. Land ownership PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 1. Displays charts showing different types of houses. 2. Asks pupils to identify and define types of houses they lived. Pupil’s Activities – Mention Types of houses in the town and villages. 3. Discuss the meaning and types of houses. Pupil’s Activities – Participate in the class discussion 4. Leads pupils to identify and state the importance of owing a house. Pupil’s Activities – State the importance of owing a house. 5. Asks pupils to state the reasons why people do not owned a house. Pupil’s Activities – State the reasons why people do not owned a house. 6. Summaries the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as teacher instructed. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. NEXT LESSON – Modern Houses and ways of making more people own houses. LESSON EVALUATION Ask pupils to: 1. explain the meaning of house. 2. state 5 types of houses. 3. mention 5 importance of owning a house. 4. state 5 reasons why many people do not own houses in Nigeria.
