Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, most pupils should have attained the following objectives – 1. Explain the term, ‘computer system’. 2. Mention the component of computer system. ENTRY BEHAVIOR The pupils can identify some of the components of computer system. INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of functional computer system. METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS Scheme of Work 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum Course Book All Relevant Material Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – MEANING OF COMPUTER SYSTEM Computer is an electronic device that accepts data as input, processes the data and gives out information. PARTS OF COMPUTER SYSTEM 1. Monitor A computer monitor is an output device that displays information in pictorial form. 2. Keyboard A keyboard is an input devices used with a computer. 3. Mouse Mouse is input device used for moving cursor around a screen. 4. Printer Printer is an output device that allows a user to print items on paper, such as letters and pictures. 5. Computer Case Computer case is a cabinet that contains most of the components of computer. 6. Motherboard Motherboard is the main circuit board inside a computer that connects the different parts of a computer together. 7. CPU CPU is known as central processing unit. 8. Hard Disk 9. RAM Memory 10. Speakers LESSON 2 – COMPONENT OF COMPUTER SYSTEM There are three major components of a computer system – 1. Hardware 2. Software 3. Users PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. Presents random parts of computer system for pupils identification. Pupil’s Activities – Identifying parts of computer. 3. Leads the pupils to describe uses of different parts of computer system. Pupil’s Activities – Describe uses of different parts of computer system. 4. Guides pupils to use keyboard (input), system unit (process) and printer (output) and explain the meaning of computer. Pupil’s Activities – Explain the meaning of computer. 5. Lists the three components of computer system. Pupil’s Activities – Mention all components of computer system. 6. Summarize the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson.
