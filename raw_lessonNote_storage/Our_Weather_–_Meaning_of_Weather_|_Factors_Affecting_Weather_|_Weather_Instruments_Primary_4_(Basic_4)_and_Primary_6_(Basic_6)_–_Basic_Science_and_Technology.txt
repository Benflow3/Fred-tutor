Introductory Activities 2. Meaning of Weather 3. Factors Affecting Weather 4. Weather Instruments 5. Lesson Evaluation and Weekly Assessment (Test) PERFORMANCE OBJECTIVES By the end of the lesson, the pupils should have attained the following objectives (cognitive, affective and psychomotor): 1. state the meaning of weather; 2. identify the factors affecting the weather. 3. relate weather conditions to changes in these factors. ENTRY BEHAVIOR The pupils can identify the present season (rainy or dry season). INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS The teacher will teach the lesson with the aid of: 1. A chart showing the weather conditions, 2. A chart showing the weather instruments; 3. Various sizes of cans, 4. Sheets of metal (aluminum) 5. Cutter, Wires, Stands, 6. Thermometer, Tracing paper, Graph sheet. 7. A chat showing the weather symbols 8. Cardboard papers 9. Coloured pencil 10. Crayons METHOD OF TEACHING Choose a suitable and appropriate methods for the lessons. Note – Irrespective of choosing methods of teaching, always introduce an activities that will arouse pupil’s interest or lead them to the lessons. REFERENCE MATERIALS 1. Scheme of Work 2. 9 – Years Basic Education Curriculum 3. Course Book 4. All Relevant Material 5. Online Information CONTENT OF THE LESSON LESSON 1 – INTRODUCTORY ACTIVITIES ACTIVITY 1 Teacher’s Activities – Ask the pupils to state the major reason for the following – 1. Bathing with warm water. 2. Drinking a chill drink. 3. Using hand fan. 4. Using umbrella. 5. Open/Close the room’s door and window. 6. Putting on/off the electric fan. Pupil’s Activities – The main reason is weather. We bath with warm water when the weather is cold, drink chill water when the weather is hot, etc. Teacher’s remark – Correct. The main reason for the above is WEATHER. Sometimes, weather determine the kinds of water (cool or warm) we used for bathing, drinking. Kinds of clothes we wear. When we are feeling heat, we open our door and windows for more ventilation. Sometimes, we close our window because the weather is cold… Ask and discuss the meaning of weather. Summary the meaning on the board. MEANING OF WEATHER Weather is the state of the atmosphere at a particular time. It is the conditions or the state of the atmosphere with respect to heat or cold, wetness or dryness, calm or storm, clearness or cloudiness. ACTIVITY 2 Teacher’s Activities – Ask the pupils, what cause us to – 1. Bathing with warm water. 2. Drinking a chill drink. 3. Using hand fan. 4. Using umbrella. 5. Open/Close the room’s door and window. 6. Putting on/off the electric fan. Pupil’s Activities – Rainfall and sunshine caused us to either bath with warm or cool water, drink chill water, etc. Teacher’s remark – Listen to the pupils and appreciate their contribution. The sun, rain, wind, etc. are factors affecting weather. Support their points, introduce and discuss factors affecting the weather. List the factors on the board. FACTORS AFFECTING WEATHER These atmospheric conditions are affected by: 1. Sun 2. Cloud 3. Wind 4. Temperature 5. Precipitation – rain , snow, dew, fog, mist 6. Moisture – water vapour 7. Humidity – amount of moisture in particular place and time. LESSON 2 – A TRIP TO METEOROLOGICAL STATION Visit a meteorological station to observe and learn how to use some standard weather instruments. MEANING AND USES OF WEATHER’S INSTRUMENTS Weather instruments include – 1. Thermometers This is used for for measuring temperature. 2. Barometers This is used for measuring pressure. 3. Anemometers This is used to for measuring wind speed. 4. Wind vanes This is used for measuring wind direction. 5. Rain gauges This is used for measuring rainfall. LESSON 3 – MAKING SOME OF THE WEATHER INSTRUMENTS Guide the pupils to make some of the weather instruments using the available improvise materials. Other Method – Our Weather – Meaning and Symbols of Weather and Weather Records (Primary 4 and 6) ASEI PDSI METHOD PRESENTATION To deliver the lesson, the teacher adopts the following steps: 1. To introduce the lesson, the teacher revises the previous lesson. Based on this, he/she asks the pupils some questions; 2. States the meaning of weather; 3. Guides pupils to discuss factors affecting weather and helps them to suggest how these factors determine weather changes. 4. Teacher’s Activities – Visit a meteorological station to observe and learn how to use some standard weather instruments. Pupil’s Activities – Take part in the trip to meteorological Station. 5. Summary the lesson on the board. Pupil’s Activities – Copy as the teacher writes. CONCLUSION To conclude the lesson for the week, the teacher revises the entire lesson and links it to the following week’s lesson. NEXT LESSON Our Weather – Meaning and Symbols of Weather and Weather Records (Primary 4 and 6) LESSON EVALUATION Ask pupils to: 1. define weather. 2. list two factors affecting the weather. 3. describe the effect of changes in the factors. 4. mention 3 weather instruments and their uses.
