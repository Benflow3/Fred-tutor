###Cultural and Creative Art | Primary/Basic 2 | Third Term
Week: 1-2
Topic: READINESS TEST -COLORING -FUNCTIONAL ARTWORKS
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: i. Enumerate several functional art works in/outside the classroom. e.g. mat, pot, basket, chair, etc.; ii. draw at least three functional art works; iii. apply suitable colours on the 0bject drawn.

Topic: FESTIVALS
Objectives: the end of the lesson pupils ould be able to: i. explain what is festival; ii. list enmples of festivals and where they are practiced; iii. enumerate the things associated with festivals.
