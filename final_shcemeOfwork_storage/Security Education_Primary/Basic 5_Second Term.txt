###Security Education | Primary/Basic 5 | Second Term
Week: 1
Topic: Student readiness assessment and revision of 1st term work
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: i) evaluate the 1st term work and treating the 1st term past questions, ii) Responds to various questions,
Week: 2
Topic: -Definition and meaning of govemment
Objectives: By the end of the lesson. pupils should be to: I) identify the followings as an agent of personal security, i) parents, ii) School, iii) community; 2) discuss their views on the above mentioned agents
Week: 3
Topic: Roles of parent on child’s personal security
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to: i) list the roles of parents on child’s personal security, ii) explain different roles of parents on child’s personal security.
Week: 4
Topic: Roles of peer group on child’s personal security
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to : i) define the term “Peer group”, ii) highlight the influence of peer group on child’s personal security.
Week: 5
Topic: Reasons for personnal security
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to i. explain reasons for personnal security ii. enumerate the importance of personnal security
Week: 6
Topic: Duties and responsibilities of a child in respect of personnal security
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to i. explain the word responsibility ii. classify the duty of a child in respect to personnal security
