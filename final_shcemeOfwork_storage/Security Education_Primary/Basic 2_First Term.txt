###Security Education | Primary/Basic 2 | First Term
Week: 1
Topic: Sources of danger and insecurity
Objectives: By the end of this lesson, pupils should be able to: i. explain what danger is ii. differntiate between danger and insecurity iii.identify various sources of insecurity in Nigeria
Week: 2
Topic: Sources of danger and insecurity Bombing I
Objectives: By the end of this lesson, pupils should be able to: i. explain bombing ii. outline the negative effect of bombing explosion in the environment
Week: 3
Topic: Sources of Danger and Insecurity Bombin Il
Objectives: By the end of this lesson, pupils should be able to: i. explain the cons uences of involving in suicide bombing ii. discuss solutions to bomb explosion in Nigeria.
Week: 4
Topic: Sources of danger and Insecurity -suspicious movement
Objectives: By the end of this lesson, pupils should be able to: i. identify suspicious persons and activities in the school, ii. highlight what to do in case of any suspected movement in school and at home.
Week: 5
Topic: Sources of Insecurity
Objectives: By the end of this lesson, pupils should be able to: i. explain food poisoning ii. identify types and symptoms of food poisoning iii.enumerate the effect of food poisoning
Week: 6
Topic: Sources of danger
Objectives: By the end of this lesson, pupils should be able to: i. identify dangerous chemicals such as acid, air asbestos, ii.dangerous chemicals on health

Objectives: MID TERM BREAK
Week: 8
Topic: Sources of danger and insecurity -Riot
Objectives: By the end of this lesson, pupils should be able to i.define riot ii. explain causes of riot iii. discuss how riot can be curbed in school
Week: 9
Topic: Sources of Danger and Insecurity: Stranger
