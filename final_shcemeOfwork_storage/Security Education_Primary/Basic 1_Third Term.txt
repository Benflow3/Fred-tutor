###Security Education | Primary/Basic 1 | Third Term
Week: 1
Topic: Revision of 2nd term work
Objectives: -what the general precaution measure is all about.
Week: 2
Topic: Concept of security road accident
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to; i. Mention cause of road accident; ii. State ways of reducing accident on the road.
Week: 3
Topic: Sources of danger: fire outbreak
Objectives: By the end of the lesson pupils should be able to; i. mention the causes of fire outbreak ii. describe the possible places fire outbreak could occur.
Week: 4
Topic: Sources of danger: relationship with strangers
Objectives: By the end of the lesson pupils should be able to; i. define relationship ii. describe and identify a stranger iii. take precautionary measures when dealing with strangers
Week: 5
Topic: Sources of Danger -Eating Contaminated Food.
Objectives: By the end of the lesson; pupils should be able to: (l) mention different type of foods (ii) identify contaminated foods e.g. Expired food in cans, food not prepared hygienically.
Week: 6
Topic: Sources of fake drugs
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to; i. define drug ii. mention the danger of taking drugs
Week: 7
Topic: MID TERM BREAK/ MID TERM TEST
Objectives: MID TERM BREAK/ MID TERM TEST
Week: 8
Topic: Sources of danger: -Drug Abuse
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to; i. define drug abuse; ii. state how drugs can be abused
Week: 9
Topic: Sources of Danger. -Gang(s)
Objectives: By the end of the lesson, pupils should be; i. identify gangs; ii. mention the effect and danger of joining gang(s)
